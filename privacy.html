Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 12, 2026

Derezz Digital LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Busy Bees mobile application and website. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information of our users.

1. Information We Collect

Local App Data: Busy Bees is designed with a privacy-first, local storage architecture. Your family's events, schedules, tasks, routines, rewards, and custom lists are stored locally on your device using an offline SQLite database. We do not transmit or store your personal calendar or household data on external servers.

Subscription & Purchases: We use RevenueCat to process in-app purchases and subscriptions. RevenueCat collects anonymous app user IDs and purchase history to verify your subscription status. Payment processing is handled directly by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play Store).

Support Communications: If you contact us at support@derezzdigital.com, we collect your email address and any details you provide solely to assist you with your inquiry.

2. Data Usage & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We do not use third-party tracking or advertising SDKs within the application.

3. Children's Privacy

Busy Bees is designed for family organization. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children. All data created within the app remains stored locally on the primary device.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
