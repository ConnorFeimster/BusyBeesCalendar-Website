Everything You Need Right At Your Fingertips

Designed to take the chaos out of managing a household with clear schedules, assigned responsibilities, star rewards, and custom lists.

Keep everyone’s schedules in one convenient place. Create events, set reminders, and easily see what’s coming up so nothing gets forgotten.
